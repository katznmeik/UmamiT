# UmamiTea

**Better tea. Deeper moments.**

UmamiTea is an Android app for brewing tea well, with a special focus on
umami-rich teas such as gyokuro, kabusecha, sencha and matcha. It brings
together:

- **Collection:** the teas you own, with origin, harvest, stock and freshness.
- **Brewing guidance:** suggested parameters for 25 tea styles, a ratio
  calculator and a water-cooling helper.
- **Steep timer:** handles several infusions in a row and rings even when the
  phone is locked.
- **Journal:** ratings, taste profile, tasting notes and photos, plus
  "Your Tea Journey" statistics.

The app works fully offline. It has no account, no ads, no tracking and no
internet access.
