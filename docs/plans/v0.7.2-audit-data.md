## Checked and left out

- **The two average-price calculations** (`StockSummary.of`, `Stock.kt:34–39`, in `Double`; `costCents`, `RoomSessionRepositories.kt:350–356`, in `BigDecimal`): the same idea, but merging them could move a rounding in a stored `cost_cents` or a shown price. Behaviour, so out of scope.
- **`EMPTY_GRAMS = 0.001`** (`RoomSessionRepositories.kt:285`) next to `StockLevel.EMPTY_BELOW_GRAMS = 0.05`: two different thresholds on purpose (a new package after an empty stock vs. the „leer" label); not a duplicate.
- **The German collator** (`Collator.getInstance(Locale.GERMAN)`, `SECONDARY`) is built in `CatalogBrowser.kt:18`, `CatalogTaste.kt:18`, `CollectionBrowser.kt:17`, `GuideRepository.kt:43` and `feature/teaware/.../TeawareViewModel.kt:72`. One shared instance would be used from several threads at once; `Collator` is not documented as safe for that. Left as is.
- **`RecipeDao.stepCount`** (`CollectionDaos.kt:187`, only `TeaAndRecipeRepositoryTest:232`) and **`ActiveBrewDao.clear`** (`SessionDaos.kt:333`, only `core:timer`'s `ControllerHarness:156`): test-only Room queries; a query can't move to the test sources, and removing them would weaken those tests (the same rule as `CatalogDao.refreshLog` in the catalog audit).
- **The `NONE` repositories and default constructor arguments** (`CatalogFoldRepository.NONE`, `CatalogBookmarkRepository.NONE`, `GuideRepository.NONE`; `OpenedDateChanges()` in `RoomSessionRepositories.kt:46`): test seams, kept as the catalog audit kept the `@Inject` secondary constructors.
- **`JournalRepository.observeStepTastings`' default body** (`JournalRepositories.kt:56–57`): Room overrides it, the anonymous fakes of evidence §5 rely on it. Kept.
- **The seed markers' two paths** (`SettingsRepository.markSeed…` three times, `SeedTeaLinkMarker` for the fourth): uneven, but it sits on the DataStore markers of 05 §6 and merging gains about ten lines. Out by risk.
- **`SettingsDataSource`** (`toModel` / `toStored`, the two migrations, the serializer): read in full; nothing to remove without touching a key or `schemaVersion`.
- **The entity ↔ model mappers** (`Mappers.kt`, `SessionMappers.kt`, the 28-field `ActiveBrewSnapshot` pair): long, but each line is one column; no shorter form without reflection.
- **`SeedPhotoDownloader.failureCause`** (`:177–184`): the stop-cause mapping the catalog audit already left out (rule 7).
- **`PhotoTranscoder.transcodePicked`** (`:122–131`) and `acceptableBounds` (`:155–164`): similar bounds checks with different limits and format rules on purpose (a picked photo vs. a shop image).
