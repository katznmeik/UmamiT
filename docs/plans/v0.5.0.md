## Local gate

Environment, once per container (as `v0.4.0-resume.md` §3):

```bash
mkdir -p ~/.gradle && grep -q in-process ~/.gradle/gradle.properties 2>/dev/null \
  || echo "kotlin.compiler.execution.strategy=in-process" >> ~/.gradle/gradle.properties
```

Every Gradle call, by the orchestrator and every implementer, goes through `gw.sh` in the session scratchpad: `flock /tmp/umamit-gradle.lock ./gradlew "$@"`. The full gate, in sequential parts:

```bash
./gradlew spotlessCheck detekt checkManifestPermissions
./gradlew --max-workers=1 testDebugUnitTest
./gradlew --max-workers=1 lint
./gradlew --max-workers=1 verifyRoborazziDebug
./gradlew assembleDebugAndroidTest
```

- A part killed for memory (exit 137) is not a test failure: rerun that part alone.
- Never `pkill java`; stop your own daemon with `./gradlew --stop`. Never delete `/tmp/robolectric-*`.
- Robolectric fetches its runtime jar at test time; on a network hiccup rerun once.

## Guards that bite in this release

- **Frozen schemas:** `umamit.db` v1–v4 and `catalog.db` v1 are frozen; #197 adds v5 next to them (`scripts/check-frozen-schemas.sh`).
- **Stored names:** `TimerSlot` names, alarm names and request codes (101–107, 201–207) never change; `BREW_2` adds 301–307 and `BREW2.`.
- **Migration:** a timer running, waiting or alerting during the update keeps its state and alerts on time; one transaction; never destructive.
- **Accuracy run, three variants:** every rc and the final need a PASS of `standard`, `alongside` and `two-brews` on their commit (full SHA, `api-level` 36, `steps` 50, `inject-delay-ms` 0). Targets p95 ≤ 250 ms and max ≤ 1000 ms (09 §4a).
- **Rule 8:** nothing may delay, hide or silence an alarm; a held alert always rings; never two sounds at once.
- **Rule 7 and D-016:** the live checks of #203, #205, #206 stay at ≤ 2 req/s on `www.tee-kontor-kiel.de` and record structure only; fixtures are synthetic.
