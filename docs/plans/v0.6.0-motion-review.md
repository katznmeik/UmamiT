# Motion review: v0.6.0 (tasting: stars, session page, taste, descriptors, editor, journal search, tea screens)

Read-only review of `git diff origin/main...HEAD` (`feature/journal`, `core/ui`, `feature/collection`, `app`) against
`docs/specs/03-ux-ui.md` §3.6 (tokens in `UmamiMotion`, D-041 calm and functional, D-055 reduce motion) and the
`/review-animations` standards translated to Compose. Rule 8 first: nothing here touches `core/timer`,
`core/timer-engine`, `feature/brew`, or how the „Gespeichert" sheet times out or yields to an alert (its hold, 10 s
timer, `alerting` early return and `AlertGated` sheets are unchanged and not proposed for change). PO rule for this
release: every tasting input is optional, so no motion may mark an empty rating, taste or notes input. The diff adds
no pulse, shake, highlight or auto-focus on any of them (checked: `RatingStars`, `TasteSlider`, `NotesSection`,
`TasteSection`, `SessionDescriptorsSection` have no `animate*` and no `LaunchedEffect` that moves focus or scroll).

New motion code is small: `AnimatedVisibility` in `SessionTasteSection.kt` and `SavedSheet.kt`, two divider fades,
the pager glide and the dot colour in `PhotoHero.kt`. Everything uses `UmamiTheme.motion` tokens except M4.

Totals: fix 2, optional 1, leave 11. Sections 2 and 3 hold the answer to item 4 and the PO questions.

## 2. Item 4: reduce motion on the GESCHMACK fold (spring snaps, fade stays)

**Right; keep it, do not remove the fade.**

- On the device, reduce motion is animator scale 0 and Compose then ends every animation, fades included, on its
  first frame (03 §3.6, `Reduce motion` paragraph, D-055). The fold is instant there: spring and fade together.
- The 100 ms `fade()` survives only on the in-app path (`reduceMotion = true` at scale 1, which tests force). That is
  the token as specified: „fades shrink to 100 ms", and STANDARDS §a11y says reduced motion is gentler, not zero:
  keep opacity and colour, drop movement. The spring is movement (height), so it snaps; the fade is opacity, so it
  stays short.
- The fade is not even visible as a lag: with the height snapped, the leaving „Geschmack hinzufügen" row is clipped
  to zero height at once and the inputs show at full size with a 100 ms opacity ramp, not a pop.
- Removing the fade would make the in-app path differ from every other fade in the app (route, sheet, divider) for no
  gain. 03 line 1349 („no animation with reduce motion") should be read as „no movement"; if the PO wants it literal,
  that is a one-line spec wording change, not a code change (Q2).

## 3. Questions for the PO

1. **Radar position (M1).** The radar appears above the sliders once three are set, and pushes them down. M1 only
   eases that shift. Alternatives, which change the design (03, SCR-16): (a) radar below the six sliders (nothing
   moves under the finger), (b) reserve its space from the start (a blank 240 dp in a mostly empty form). Keep the
   radar on top with the eased shift (M1), or change the layout?
2. **Wording of the reduce-motion rule for the fold.** Keep the spec line as is (movement snaps, the 100 ms fade of the
   in-app path stays, instant on the device), or reword 03 line 1349 to say so? (No behaviour change either way.)
