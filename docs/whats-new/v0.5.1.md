## What's new in 0.5.1

**Improved**
- Start shows one list, *Zuletzt gebrüht*, with your six latest teas; a tap brews that tea again.
- On the tea detail, *Knapp ab* shows the value in effect.
- The *Katalog* filter sheet keeps *Fertig* beside the title, and *Zurücksetzen* sits at the end.
- New display font, Alegreya Sans, for titles, tea names and timer digits.
