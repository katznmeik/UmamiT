## What's new in 0.7.0

**New**
- *Statistik*, "Deine Tee-Reise": an overview, ratings and consumption of your tea drinking.
- Pick any span of months for the statistics, and fold sections you don't need.
- A donut chart of your teas, a *Tee-Rangliste* and *Neu entdeckt* for newly tried teas.
- Consumption in litres, with "€ pro Liter" in the statistics and on the tea detail.

**Improved**
- The *Knapp* chip shows how much is left, for example "14 g · knapp".
