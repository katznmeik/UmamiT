## What's new in 0.2.0

**New**
- The *Katalog* holds Tee-Kontor Kiel's green teas, refreshed on demand with *Katalog laden*; new teas are marked *Neu*.
- Search and filter the teas; tea pages show photos, sizes, prices and a recipe.
- Add a catalog tea to *Meine Tees*, or buy it again, with the exact price fetched when you add it.
- *Im Shop öffnen* jumps to the tea's shop page.
- The *Ratgeber* collects the shop's tea guides.
- Pick a tea from the catalog when starting a brew, and check its recipe with *Rezept prüfen* or *Rezept passt*.

**Fixed**
- The alert sounds are ready as soon as a timer runs, so the first alert after a cold start is no longer late.
