## What's new in 0.1.0

**New**
- Steep green tea in several infusions: *Start* begins each step, a *Vorwarnung* ticks shortly before it ends, and the alert sounds on time, even with the screen off or the app closed.
- The timer shows in a Live Update and over the lock screen; *Pause*, *Weiter* and *Abgegossen* work from there.
- The *Schritte-Sheet* lets you change each infusion's time and temperature, or add and delete steps, for the current session.
- A quick timer for anything else, and *Nochmal brühen* to repeat a tea in one tap.
- Two teas to start with, Bio Sencha Tanabata and Bio Sencha Hisui, plus two vessels, with leaf amount and water temperature hints.
- A first-start setup checks notifications, battery and alarm permissions; *Diagnose* shows how punctual the alerts were.
- Five alert sounds to try with *Alarm testen*; the *Journal* lists every session automatically.
