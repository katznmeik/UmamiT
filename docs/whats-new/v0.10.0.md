## What's new in 0.10.0

**New**
- English as a second app language: *Einstellungen* › *Darstellung* › *Sprache* (System · Deutsch · English). Numbers, dates and the CSV export follow it; tea names, the tea vocabulary and the shop's texts stay German.
- A six-page tour of the main features on the first start, and any time from *Einstellungen* › *Hilfe* › *Einführung*.
- Every entry in a tea's Vorrat history can be edited: a purchase's date, grams and price, a correction's amount and day. Editing or deleting a purchase recomputes the tea's past session costs, and Statistik follows.
- After an update, the sheet „Vollbild-Alarm wieder erlauben" asks you once to turn the full-screen alarm back on when Android has switched it off.

**Changed**
- The timer and its alert stay upright when the phone is held sideways, also over the lock screen; the other screens turn as before.
