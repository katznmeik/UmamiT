## What's new in 0.9.0

**New**
- Back up all your data, photos included, to one file, and restore it on this or a new phone (*Daten & Sicherung*).
- Undo an import for 7 days: a safety copy is made first.
- Export the journal as a CSV table for Excel.
- A monthly reminder to back up once 20 new sessions have piled up.
- Share a recipe with others as a file, and take one over from a shared file.
- *Alle Daten löschen*: start over as after a fresh install.

**Improved**
- The tabs' ⋮ menu gives way when an alert appears.
