## What's new in 0.9.0

**New**
- Back up all your data, photos included, to one file and restore it on this or a new phone (*Daten & Sicherung*), with a size estimate and a progress bar.
- Undo an import for 7 days: a safety copy is made first, and the undo tells you what you would lose.
- „Letzte Sicherung" shows when you last backed up and how many sessions are new since; a monthly reminder (a switch, on) nudges you once 20 have piled up.
- Export the journal as a CSV table for Excel.
- Share a recipe as text with a file attached (`<Tee>.umamit-rezept.json`); tap the received file, share it to UmamiT, or use Meine Tees ⋮ *Rezept importieren*, and take it over with the matching tea already picked. The shop page loads only when you tap it.
- *Alle Daten löschen*: start over as after a fresh install.

**Changed**
- A fresh install and *Alle Daten löschen* start empty: no sample teas, no preset vessels and no photo download at first start. Teas and vessels already on your phone stay.

**Fixed**
- The tabs' ⋮ menu no longer stays open over an alert.
