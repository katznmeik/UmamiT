## What's new in 0.4.1

**New**
- *Nachkauf erfassen* for every tea; a purchase also brings back an archived tea.
- Freshness: opening and best-before dates, freshness labels, and *Rückgängig* when a pack was marked as opened.
- Stock history per tea, with delete.
- Cost: price per 100 g, a cost estimate per recipe and the real average per cup.
- Your own photos for teas (*Als Titelbild*, *Löschen*) and a photo for each vessel.
- *Aus Katalog zurücksetzen* restores a tea to its catalog values.

**Improved**
- Settings closes when you switch tabs.
