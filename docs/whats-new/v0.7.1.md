## What's new in 0.7.1

**New**
- Rate each session for the tea (*Tee*) and the brewing (*Zubereitung*) separately; your earlier ratings count for both.
- *Journal* rows and the tea detail show both ratings; *Statistik* can rank by either and shows both in the history.

**Improved**
- Smoother transitions throughout the app, especially when you unlock the phone and return to it.
- Leaving the timer on a locked phone shows only the lock screen, with no flash of Start.
