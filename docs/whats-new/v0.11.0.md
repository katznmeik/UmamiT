## What's new in 0.11.0

**New**
- *Anleitungen*: eight short how-tos with numbered steps and a picture of the screen, under *Einstellungen* › *Hilfe* › *Anleitungen*: adding a tea without the catalog or from a shop link, stock and cost, recipes, brewing, journal and taste, data and backup, and a reliable alarm.
- *Hilfe* in the ⋮ menu of Sammlung, a tea, *Rezept bearbeiten*, Journal, a session and *Daten & Sicherung* opens the Anleitung for that screen.

**Changed**
- The tour is reworked: each of its six pages shows a picture of the screen with the important spot marked, a little more text, and „Mehr dazu" links to the matching Anleitung; back returns to the same place in the tour.
- The tour's page dots blend as you swipe, *Weiter* and *Fertig* keep one width, and the last page shows only *Fertig*.
