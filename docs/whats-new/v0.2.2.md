## What's new in 0.2.2

**New**
- *Merken* teas in the *Katalog* and find them again in your *Merkliste* (*Gemerkt*).
- The quick timer has its own *Vorwarnung* choice and an 8:00 chip.
- Teas that run low are marked *Knapp* from 15 g, with a card on Start.
- Tea groups in the tea picker.

**Improved**
- Leaf amount and water show on every READY screen.
- A catalog notification now opens the refresh sheet.
