## What's new in 0.6.0

**New**
- Rate sessions with stars, on the session page and the „Gespeichert" sheet, and add notes in place.
- *Geschmack hinzufügen*: six taste sliders with a radar, and *Beschreibungen* with a searchable list of descriptors.
- Photos on a session page; without your own, the tea's cover shows.
- ⋮ *Bearbeiten* corrects a session: date, vessel, leaf amount, water, step times and temperatures.
- *Zeiten ins Rezept übernehmen*: adopt your shortened step times into the recipe.
- *Journal*: search, a rating row, and filters for *Tee*, *Bewertung* and *Mit Notizen*.
- Tea detail and *Meine Tees* show the average rating, taste profile and last sessions; sort by *Bewertung*.

**Improved**
- A slimmer, more elegant handle on the taste sliders and the *Lautstärke* slider.
