## What's new in 0.5.0

**New**
- Two timers at once: two brews, or a brew and the quick timer; a third start asks *Welchen Timer beenden?*.
- The whole Tee-Kontor range in the *Katalog*: black, white, oolong, Pu-Erh, Rotbusch, fruit tea and chai, with nine kind chips.
- Flavour filter in the *Katalog*: taste notes and taste scales.
- A per-tea stock warning (*Warnung*, *Knapp ab*) and a reminder interval from daily to weekly or off.

**Improved**
- With two alarms due, the second rings as soon as the first has stopped sounding.
