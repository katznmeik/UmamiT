## What's new in 0.8.0

**New**
- A light timer: the timer screens now follow *Design*, with a sage alarm in the light app.
- Taste sliders that fit the kind of tea, with an info section, "Was bedeuten die Regler?".
- *Einstellungen* › *Katalog* shows the progress and result of a catalog refresh.

**Improved**
- "Session" and "aufgießen" replace "Sitzung" and "brühen" everywhere; the setup is titled "Neue Session".
- Tapping a tea in *Tee wählen* flows smoothly into the setup.
- The ⋮ menu sits at its button on every tab.
