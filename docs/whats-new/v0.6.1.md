## What's new in 0.6.1

**New**
- Rate the taste and add a note for each infusion, on the timer screen and on the session page (*Geschmack*).
- Quick timer chips for 1:00, 2:00, 3:00, 5:00, 8:00, 10:00 and 15:00.
- Open a tea photo full screen, with pinch and double-tap zoom and swipe to the next photo.
- *Tee ansehen* and *Im Katalog ansehen* in the ⋮ menu of a session and a tea.
- *Design* in *Darstellung*: choose light, dark or follow the phone.

**Improved**
- The *Katalog* opens with every kind of tea, grouped by kind, and shows "N Tees".
- A tea's taste radar now averages all its infusions.
