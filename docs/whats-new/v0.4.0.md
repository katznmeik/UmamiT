## What's new in 0.4.0

**New**
- *Meine Tees*: your own tea collection with search, *Kategorie* and *Sorte* filters and a remembered sort.
- Tea detail and tea editor: add teas from the catalog or without it, and archive or delete them.
- Recipe editor: reorder steps by dragging, three step kinds, a *Vorwarnung* per step and several recipes per tea.
- Teaware in *Einstellungen*, with *Neues Gefäß …* in the brew setup.
- A session page for finished brews, with *So nochmal brühen*.
- Delete journal entries (*Sitzung löschen?*); *Tee hinzufügen* now sits in the ⋮ menu of *Sammlung*.
- A *Grüntee* chip in the *Katalog*.

**Fixed**
- *Journal* rows show the first infusion's own temperature.
