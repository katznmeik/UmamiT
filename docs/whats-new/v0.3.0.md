## What's new in 0.3.0

**New**
- Run a quick timer alongside a brew: the other timer gets its own row, and both show in the bar.
- A daily reminder when teas run low, at a time you set.
- *Lautstärke* for the *Vorwarnung* and the alert, on the alarm volume.
- The *Katalog* groups fold with a tap, and a tea's menu offers *Im Katalog ansehen*.

**Improved**
- Hot steps of 10 to 59 minutes now get a *Vorwarnung* and a countdown in the Live Update.
- READY shows how much of the tea is left.
- Tap a step's time to pick the duration; hold − or + to count faster.

**Fixed**
- Alerts now ring under "Nicht stören" when it lets alarms through (*Bei „Nicht stören" klingeln*, on by default).
