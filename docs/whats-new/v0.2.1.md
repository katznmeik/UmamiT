## What's new in 0.2.1

**New**
- A *Vorwarnung* for every infusion, set per step in the *Schritte-Sheet* and kept with the recipe.
- Four new *Vorwarnton* sounds, chosen in the settings and tried with *Anhören*.
- Herbal teas (*Kräutertee*) appear in the *Katalog* with their own filter.
- Add a tea from a shop link you share or paste into UmamiT.
- Correct a tea's stock with "Wie viel ist noch da?", and remove a tea from the tea picker, with undo.

**Improved**
- The timer's back and menu buttons stay readable on any photo, and the top shows an ink-wash band with the tea photo as a small seal.
- Seven more vessels to choose from.
- Sold-out teas stay in the *Katalog*; teas that left the range are marked.
